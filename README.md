# Night Shift Rounds

A phone-first task timeline for PCNAs on a 1900–0730 night shift.

- Numbered patient slots (Pt 1–24) with per-patient tasks: vitals, tele battery, dinner check, accuchecks (AC/HS or Q6 presets, plus your own times), Q2 turns (scheduled from the last turn), bladder scans, orthostatic vitals, and weights (standing or bed).
- Rounds grouped by due time, with a "Start" time one hour early. Q2 turns and the glucometer control check run on the hour.
- Bundle care skips 0200 vitals.

## Privacy

The app stores no names, bed numbers, or patient details. Everything saves only in the browser on the phone using it, and **Clear shift** wipes it. It is a personal reminder list, not the record of care: current orders and the nurse always take priority.

## Use on iPhone

Open the GitHub Pages link in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
